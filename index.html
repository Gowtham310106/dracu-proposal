Module Specifications

Complete Module & Feature Breakdown

Detailed functional scope of all software modules designed for Dr. Bharath's Acu Heal operations.

Admin & User Role Management

Security

Comprehensive role-based security ensuring staff members only access modules relevant to their daily responsibilities across branches.

  • Secure authentication & encrypted password hashing
  • Admin, Doctor, Front-Desk & Accounts permission levels
  • Simultaneous multi-staff access without session collisions
  • Audit logging of sensitive billing and patient modifications

Multi-Branch Management (3 Branches)

Core Operations

Centralized platform connecting all 3 clinic branches with instant switching for management and isolated views for branch staff.

  • Dedicated branch-wise records for patients, appointments & sales
  • Branch identification tags across all bills, receipts, and reports
  • Branch-isolated front-office views to avoid operational confusion
  • Consolidated management overview for unified revenue tracking

Smart Patient Registration & Lookup

Clinical Engine

Fast, frictionless patient intake with automatic duplicate detection and integrated camera capture for patient records.

  • Automatic unique Patient ID (PID) generation
  • Live duplicate detection via mobile number with instant auto-fill
  • Webcam capture and device upload for patient profile photos
  • Comprehensive medical history, referral source, and custom fields

Appointment Scheduling & Slot Directory

Front-Office

Visual slot booking and date-wise appointment directory enabling front-office staff to schedule, reschedule, and track patient flow.

  • Custom appointment time slots and branch selection
  • Today's live appointment directory with quick status toggles
  • Fast date-navigation to view upcoming and historical bookings
  • Instant patient contact lookup and rescheduling/cancellation logs

Patient Fee & Session Package Tracker

Acupuncture Engine

Dedicated session accounting specifically tailored for multi-session acupuncture treatment packages and balance tracking.

  • Configurable per-session and total treatment package fees
  • Automatic calculation of total payable, paid, and pending balance
  • Real-time session consumption meter (Completed vs Remaining)
  • Complete historical payment and session attendance timeline

Billing, Tax Invoices & Print Layouts

Finance

Professional, clinic-branded invoice generation with automatic sequential numbering and thermal/A4 print compatibility.

  • Automatic sequential invoice numbering with branch prefixes
  • Itemized treatment, session packages, and payment breakdown
  • Customized print layout matching Dr. Bharath Clinic's brand identity
  • Instant receipt printing and PDF download for patients

Automated WhatsApp Communication

Automation

Hands-free patient engagement delivering timely appointment session reminders and personalized birthday wishes.

  • Automated session reminder notifications before scheduled visits
  • Automated personalized birthday greetings for registered patients
  • Customizable clinic message templates and reminder frequencies
  • Integrated via official WhatsApp API (usage billed pass-through)

CRM & Multi-Source Lead Pipeline

Growth

Structured lead tracking to capture enquiries from Instagram, Google, and referrals, managing the conversion funnel.

  • Channel source attribution (Instagram, Google, Walk-in, Ads, Referral)
  • Funnel status: New → Follow-up → Attended → Converted → Lost
  • Follow-up scheduling with reminders for front-desk staff
  • Branch-wise CRM conversion rates and acquisition analytics

Biometric Hardware Attendance Sync

Hardware Integration

Integration with the clinic's existing biometric attendance hardware to log staff check-ins and working hours.

  • Direct sync with biometric machine API/SDK for staff check-in/out
  • Automated calculation of daily working hours and attendance status
  • Branch-wise staff attendance reports for management review
  • Subject to device SDK/API network availability and data access

Clinic Accounts, Income & Expenses

Accounting

Straightforward daily accounting capturing treatment collections, categorized clinic expenses, and net daily positions.

  • Daily sales, collections, and outstanding balance summaries
  • Categorized expense recording (Rent, Utilities, Supplies, Misc)
  • Branch-wise income vs expense reports with date-range filters
  • Exportable financial summaries for accountant review

Vendor Directory & Purchase Ledger

Procurement

Centralized record keeping for clinic equipment, consumables, needle suppliers, and external service vendors.

  • Vendor profiles, contact details, and product categories
  • Purchase logging and payment status tracking
  • Historical procurement logs and vendor payment history

Staff Profiles & Basic Salary Management

HR & Payroll

Staff directory with branch assignments, baseline salary structures, and historical salary payment disbursement records.

  • Staff master profiles, role assignments, and branch mapping
  • Monthly salary structure configuration and payment records
  • Payment date tracking and basic staff payout history

Treatment Progress Video Directory

Visual Archives

Secure patient-linked video archive allowing therapists to capture, upload, and review treatment progress footage.

  • Patient-specific visual treatment progress history archive
  • Direct upload of rehabilitation and needle technique videos
  • 10 GB included storage under standard maintenance plan
  • Real-time storage consumption meter with transparent ₹50/GB tier

Executive Management KPI Dashboard

Analytics

Single-pane-of-glass executive overview displaying real-time financial, patient, and operational indicators across all branches.

  • Live metrics: Today's sales, collections, pending dues, and expenses
  • Real-time appointment timeline and patient check-in statuses
  • CRM lead conversion rates and top lead acquisition channels
  • Interactive custom date filters and branch comparison views
